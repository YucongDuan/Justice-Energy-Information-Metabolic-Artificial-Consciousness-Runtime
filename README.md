# DIKWP-TIANHENG-AC v1.0.0

Created by Yucong Duan (段玉聪).

DIKWP-天衡：正义-能量-信息价值代谢人工意识系统  
Justice-Energy-Information Metabolic Artificial Consciousness Runtime

TIANHENG is a generic, runnable candidate artificial-consciousness architecture. It does not treat model self-approval or predicted user satisfaction as final authority. It combines:

- native D/I/K/W/P semantic continuity;
- a recurrent, limited-capacity global workspace;
- temporal self/resource/stakeholder models;
- nine-perspective evaluation with non-compensatory truth, rights, future, and corrigibility gates;
- request-level justice friction instead of permanent “good/bad person” labels;
- measured compute energy, useful information yield, downstream energy dividend, and human-attention cost;
- non-transferable, non-fungible Justice Dividend Units (JDU) minted only after observed outcomes and independent sign-off;
- appeal, correction, deterministic replay, and zero automatic external-action authority.

## Scientific boundary

The system is a functional artificial-consciousness candidate architecture. It does not establish phenomenal consciousness, metaphysical TianDao, moral infallibility, or literal creation of physical energy from information.

## Justice currency

JDU is an auditable governance receipt, not cash or cryptocurrency. Six accounts remain separate:

1. truth restoration;
2. rights protection;
3. repair and restitution;
4. commons contribution;
5. future optionality;
6. energy stewardship.

A positive account never cancels an unrepaired rights, deception, affected-party-voice, or independent-review debt.
